# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary audience is prospective employers and hiring decision-makers evaluating Sahrul for a job. The portfolio is also maintained for personal use as a concise record of projects and experience.

## Product Purpose

The portfolio presents Sahrul's projects, experience, and contact details in one place so prospective employers can understand the work and decide whether to start a conversation. Success means making the work easy to browse, credible, and memorable enough to support a job opportunity.

## Positioning

Open decision: the portfolio's strongest differentiating mechanism or positioning claim has not yet been confirmed.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive on the web, scan a single-page portfolio, open curated project folders, review a work timeline, read a short about statement, and use the contact path when they want to continue the conversation.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The current product is a Next.js and TypeScript web application.
- The experience is organized around a hero, project folders, an experience timeline, an about section, and contact navigation.
- Future work must preserve the personal portfolio purpose and support job-seeking evaluation.
- Name, contact details, experience, and project information are product content that must remain available.
- The user has expressed a preference for a minimalist, elegant, simple, and animated portfolio experience; this is recorded as a confirmed product preference, while visual implementation decisions remain outside this file.

## Evidence on Hand

The user confirmed that the portfolio has available name, contact details, experience, and project content. The repository currently contains the portfolio shell, project-folder interaction, experience timeline, and about copy in `app/page.tsx`, with supporting UI components under `app/components/` and `components/`.

## Product Principles

- Make the work easy for prospective employers to understand quickly.
- Present real projects and experience without inventing proof or claims.
- Keep the portfolio personal, focused, and easy to navigate.
- Let motion support comprehension and emphasis rather than obstructing access to content.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The web experience should remain keyboard accessible, provide visible focus states, support reduced motion preferences, and preserve semantic navigation as the portfolio evolves.
